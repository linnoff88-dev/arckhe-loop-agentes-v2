The core idea in 30 seconds

A loop = 3 things

Trigger, action, and stop condition. Underneath every complicated design, it’s always reason → act → observe, repeating until "done".

Verification is everything

A loop is only as good as the way it checks "done." Without verification, it confidently delivers the wrong work — and calls it done.

You become the architect

Instead of typing the next step, you design the system that decides the next step. That’s the “v2” leap: Loop Engineering.
